Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 • Rehnova Digitals

1. Overview

Rehnova Digitals ("we", "our") is committed to protecting client privacy and safeguarding technical disclosures. This policy outlines how information is collected, processed, and secured across our services and web properties.

2. Information Collection

We collect minimal information necessary to process technical project inquiries, schedule strategy consultations, and execute client contracts:

  • Contact details: Name, work email address, company name, and phone number.
  • Technical specifications: Project scope requirements, architecture needs, and timeline expectations.
  • Server diagnostics: IP addresses, browser signatures, and security logs required for platform protection.

3. Use of Information

Data is used exclusively for evaluating technical proposals, executing Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), delivering engineering support, and communicating project updates. We do not sell, rent, or trade client information to any third party.

4. Confidentiality & Security

All project disclosures, codebase access, and client communications are governed under NDA-first confidentiality standards. Data is protected with encrypted transmission (TLS/SSL) and restricted access controls.

5. Inquiries & Data Requests

For questions regarding this policy or to request data removal, contact our team directly at contact@rehnova.digital.
